# AI artwork, music and coding disclosure — Moonbound V1.4

The project contains AI-generated and AI-assisted content:

- Room, hull, and module artwork in `assets/*.webp` is user-supplied AI-generated Moonbound concept art, adapted for the browser.
- The three mission header images (`assets/mission_crop*.png`) are cropped from a user-approved AI-generated interface concept. These are **illustrations**, not NASA photographs or authenticated mission renders.
- Six short background pieces (`assets/classical_*.mp3`) are new, original gentle piano-and-strings arrangements synthesized for the game, with no copyrighted commercial audio samples or existing composition playback.
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript, documentation and testing were AI-assisted with direction, review, and contributions from the project team.
- NASA and JPL references are attributed as scientific reference data, separately from fabricated gameplay values. NASA does not endorse or sponsor this independent educational game.
